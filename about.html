About CalorieTrack

A simple and easy-to-use food calorie tracking application designed to help you understand and monitor your daily food intake.

🍎

What is CalorieTrack?

Your simple companion for daily food tracking.

CalorieTrack is a mini web application that allows users to calculate calories, record their meals, and monitor their daily food intake.

Users can select a food from the available food database or manually enter a food and its calorie value. The application then stores the meal information in the browser.

🍽️

🚀 How It Works

Track your meals in a few simple steps.

1

Choose Your Food

Select a food from the available list or manually add one.

2

Enter Quantity

Enter the amount of food you consumed.

3

Calculate Calories

The application calculates the calorie value for the selected quantity.

4

Track Your Day

Add the food to your meal and monitor your daily calorie intake.

5

Check Your History

Review your previous days and compare your calorie intake.

Our Goal

The goal of CalorieTrack is to make basic calorie tracking simple, accessible, and easy to understand while demonstrating how HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and LocalStorage can work together to create a functional web application.
